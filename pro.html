What each Pro feature does

New streams, processed

PeakSense spots a new stream and clips it, so you wake up to clips ready to review.

Scheduled posting

Set it once and it renders and uploads while you sleep, work or stream, at the hours your audience shows up.

Recaps

A whole stream cut down to its best stretches as one long video, with YouTube chapters written for you.

Stream Wrapped

Your month of streams as a video and a page to share, made on its own.

Live capture

Raids, redemptions, bits and chat spikes become clips on Twitch without a button, and a big moment can go out before you end.

A second look

A few hours after processing it runs again and adds only moments you haven't clipped.

Learns from your winners

Reads how your posts did and leans toward moments like your best ones.

Title testing

Each Short tries two titles and keeps the one that pulled more views.

Morning report

One message a day with last night's clips, what posted and how it's doing.

Your brand, everywhere

Your logo, caption style, cards and layout on everything made for you.
