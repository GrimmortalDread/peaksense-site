HOW IT WORKS

From raw recording to posted clip, four steps.

No editing timeline, no manual cropping, no remembering to hit publish.

01

Record

Keep streaming or recording like normal — PeakSense picks up your VODs automatically.

02

Detect

It scans the footage for the moments most likely to land — kills, clutches, reactions, chat spikes.

03

Edit

Each moment is trimmed and reframed for vertical formats, with facecam layout handled for you.

04

Post

Review it, or let your posting schedule take over — clips go out to YouTube and TikTok on their own.
