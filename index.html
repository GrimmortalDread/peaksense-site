Where the AI comes from
HuggingFace

The AI behind the curtain.

HuggingFace hosts the open-source AI models PeakSense uses. The one that matters here is pyannote/speaker-diarization-3.1. Speaker diarization is the AI task of answering "who spoke when" — it splits your stream's audio into segments and tags each one with the person talking. Paste a free read-only token once, accept the model's license, and PeakSense downloads it to your computer; after that it never needs HuggingFace again.

  • What diarization does — splits your audio into segments and labels each one with who's speaking, so a transcript reads like a conversation instead of a wall of text.
  • How PeakSense uses it — it runs after transcription and tags every line with a speaker, powering "Know who's talking": find a guest's best reaction, or pull the back-and-forths that made the stream.
  • Downloads pyannote/speaker-diarization-3.1 — the leading open model for speaker separation, running locally on your PC.
  • One-time setup, then offline — accept the model's license, paste a free read token, and PeakSense pulls the model down. It never calls home again.
  • Completely optional — it's only needed for speaker separation on co-streams and podcasts. Captions, detection, and face finding all work without it.

Common questions

Things people ask before they try it

How much does PeakSense cost?

The free version is the full toolset — highlight detection, facecam layouts, captions, sequence mode, playlist bulk processing — used manually. PeakSense Pro is a one-time $69 payment that adds the automation: scheduled auto-posting, live auto-capture, the learning loop, priority queue, no clip cap, and custom branding. No subscription, no per-clip charges.

Does PeakSense upload my videos anywhere?

No. Everything — download, detection, clipping, captioning — runs on your computer. The only time PeakSense touches the internet is when it's pulling a stream you asked for or posting a clip to your own channel.

What kind of computer do I need?

A Windows PC. If you have an NVIDIA GPU, PeakSense uses it and runs fast. If you don't, it uses your CPU and just runs slower. No special hardware required either way.

How does the license work?

You get a key that starts with PS-. Paste it into Settings and you're done. No subscription, no monthly renewal, no calls to our servers to verify it. It just works.

Is the free version a real product or a trial?

It's the real product. Every feature works in the free version — you just do the clicking yourself. Pro is for people who want autopilot.

What's the story with TikTok posting?

TikTok's Content Posting API restricts apps that haven't completed an audit yet. Until PeakSense finishes that audit, TikTok posts land as private (self-only) rather than going public. We'll say it plainly in the dashboard rather than pretend otherwise.

How is this different from other clip tools?

Two things. First, it runs on your computer — your footage never gets uploaded to someone else's servers. Second, there are no credits. You can process as many streams as your hardware can handle. Most cloud tools cap you at a few minutes per month; PeakSense doesn't.
