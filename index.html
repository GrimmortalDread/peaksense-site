Before, during and after the stream

Most tools start once the VOD exists. PeakSense starts with how your stream looks and keeps going until the clips have been seen.

Before you go live

Build your look once: logo, colours and fonts, then starting soon, BRB and ending screens, panels, emotes and sub badges made from it. The screens drop straight into OBS. When you hit Go Live, a live card goes out to your Discord and your Instagram story.

Your brand

While you're live

PeakSense records through OBS and marks moments as they happen. Mark one yourself with a hotkey, a Stream Deck button, !clip in chat or your phone. With Pro, raids, tips and channel points get caught too, and a big moment can be a Short before you've even ended.

Finding the moments

After you end it

Captioned clips with your facecam on top, montages, a full recap with YouTube chapters, thumbnails and titles. Then a schedule that posts at the hours your viewers show up, and numbers that tell you what to make next.

Posting and growing

One app for all of it

No exporting between tools, no browser tabs, no monthly bill. Home shows what came out of your last stream and what's going out next.

What one stream turns into

A four-hour stream is a week of posts if somebody does the cutting. PeakSense does the cutting, and the rest.

Review over coffee

New clips play full screen, one after another. One key keeps it, queues it, bins it or opens it in the editor.

  • Each clip says why it was picked
  • Stars and game tags for later
  • The same on your phone with a swipe

It watches the stream with you

Connect OBS once, with no plugin. While you're live, PeakSense records and marks moments as they happen. When you stop, the recording is already sorted.

  • Chat spikes, loud moments, hype lines and funny lines
  • Kills, clutches, wins and boss fights read from your game's HUD and sounds
  • Raids, tips, bits and channel points as moments of their own (Twitch, StreamElements, Streamlabs)

Layouts that find your webcam

Facecam on top and gameplay below, a corner cam, a split screen or a two-person duo. Your webcam is found for you and stays lined up for the whole stream.

  • Drag the webcam or the gameplay to frame it yourself
  • Zooms and camera moves you set on the timeline
  • A crop that follows the action, with Pro
