PEAKSENSE — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

This policy covers the PeakSense desktop app for Windows ("PeakSense", "the app") and the website peaksense.net. PeakSense is made by an independent developer ("we", "us"). Contact: support@peaksense.net.

The short version
  • PeakSense runs on your own PC. Your videos, recordings, transcripts, account tokens and settings stay on your computer. We don't run servers that receive them.
  • PeakSense contacts a service (YouTube, Twitch, TikTok and the others listed below) only to do something you set up or asked for, such as signing in, finding your streams, or posting your clip.
  • No analytics, no tracking, no advertising, and we never sell or share your data.
  • PeakSense uses YouTube API Services. See section 7.
  • To delete everything, see Data deletion.

1. What the app handles

Depending on the features you use, PeakSense handles the following. All of it is stored and processed on your computer.

  • Your videos: OBS recordings made while PeakSense watches your stream, video files you import or drop in (for example YouTube Studio or Twitch downloads, ShadowPlay or ReLive recordings), VODs downloaded from your own connected channel, and the clips, montages, thumbnails and captions made from them.
  • Analysis results: speech transcripts made by a speech-recognition model that runs on your PC, loudness and motion measurements, gameplay events detected from the picture and game sounds (using game profiles you create), optional speaker labels, and moments found during a live stream.
  • Account information from platforms you connect: your display name, channel or user ID and, for TikTok, your creator nickname, avatar and posting options.
  • Sign-in tokens issued by the platforms you connect (Google/YouTube, Twitch, TikTok) and, if you add them, a Hugging Face token and a Discord/Slack webhook address.
  • YouTube sign-in session (optional): if you sign in to YouTube inside PeakSense's own sign-in window to download your own channel's VODs, PeakSense keeps that window's session cookies on your PC so downloads keep working.
  • Platform data about your own channel: your recent uploads and broadcasts (titles, descriptions, tags, durations, thumbnails, dates), your playlists, and view, like and comment counts of clips you posted.
  • Live stream information (Twitch): whether you're live, your stream title and category, and events such as raids, subscriptions, bits, channel point redemptions and chat messages, including the chatting viewer's username and message text. See section 5.
  • OBS connection details: the address, port and WebSocket password of your OBS, and the file names of its recordings.
  • What you enter: titles, descriptions, captions, hashtags, schedules, presets, game profiles and settings, plus your PeakSense Pro licence key.
  • Logs: a local log file of what the app did, used for troubleshooting. Sign-in tokens and secrets are removed from logs and from the diagnostics file you can export.

PeakSense does not collect usage analytics, crash reports or advertising identifiers, and it does not read the cookies or data of your web browsers.

2. How it's used

Only to provide PeakSense's features to you:

  • finding highlight moments in your streams and cutting, captioning and reframing clips;
  • showing your recent streams and matching local recordings to your uploads;
  • checking that a video you ask PeakSense to download belongs to your own connected channel;
  • posting clips to the account you connected, as described in the Terms (manually, or on the schedule you set);
  • learning which of your posted clips performed well, so future picks lean toward similar moments;
  • looking up which game you streamed from your stream title, tags or description;
  • sending notifications to your own Discord or Slack webhook, if you set one up.

We don't use your data for advertising, profiling or training AI models, and we never see it: it stays on your PC.

3. Where it's stored and how long it's kept

Everything is stored on your computer, not by us:

  • the PeakSense data folder (%LOCALAPPDATA%\PeakSense for the installed app, and %APPDATA%\PeakSense for YouTube sign-in files), including the app's database, settings, tokens, logs and its sign-in window's profile;
  • the folders you choose for downloads, imports and the video library, your OBS recording folder, and PeakSense's clip folders;
  • short-lived temporary files in Windows' temp folder, deleted when a job finishes.

Retention:

  • Full-length videos PeakSense created or copied, such as OBS recordings it made, uploads and downloads, are moved to the Recycle Bin after the number of days you set (30 by default), or when your library goes over the size limit you set. You can pin videos to keep them, or turn the limits off. Your own imported files are only touched if you allow it.
  • YouTube statistics for your posted clips are refreshed within 30 days, or deleted if they can't be refreshed (for example, after you disconnect YouTube).
  • Sign-in tokens are deleted and revoked with the platform when you disconnect that platform in PeakSense.
  • Everything else stays until you delete it or remove PeakSense's folders (see Data deletion).

Security: the data is protected by your Windows account, the same as your other files. Sign-in tokens, the YouTube sign-in session and your OBS password are stored in PeakSense's data folder without additional encryption, so anyone who can use your Windows account could read them. Keep your PC and Windows account secure, and disconnect platforms you no longer use.

5. Live sessions (OBS and Twitch)

If you connect OBS, PeakSense watches your stream while you're live. It reads the recording OBS is writing on your PC, finds moments, and saves the recording to its local library when you stop.

With Twitch connected, it also records the time of chat messages and events. This lets it find chat spikes and lets you mark moments with a chat command. That log, including viewers' usernames and message text, is stored only on your PC next to the recording. You can delete it with the recording.

Nothing from a live session is sent to us.

6. Downloads from platforms

PeakSense downloads videos only from your own connected channel. It confirms through the YouTube or Twitch API that the video belongs to the account you connected before downloading. The exception is Kick VOD links you paste, which Kick provides no way to verify. You can turn platform downloads off in Settings → Storage and use OBS recordings or imported files only.

Downloads are saved on your PC and follow the storage rules in section 3.

7. YouTube API Services

PeakSense uses YouTube API Services to show your channel's streams and playlists, check a video's owner, upload and schedule your clips, and read statistics of clips you posted. By using PeakSense's YouTube features you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

  • Accessed: your channel ID and name; your uploads, live VODs and playlists (titles, descriptions, tags, durations, thumbnails, dates, owner); statistics (views, likes, comments) of clips posted through PeakSense.
  • Used for: the features above only.
  • Stored: on your PC only. Statistics are refreshed or deleted within 30 days, and sign-in tokens are kept until you disconnect.
  • Shared: with no one. Nothing is sent to us or to other parties.
  • Revoking access: disconnect YouTube in PeakSense, which revokes the token, or remove PeakSense at Google security settings.

PeakSense's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

8. TikTok

PeakSense uses TikTok's Login Kit and Content Posting API to post your clips to the TikTok account you connect. It reads your creator nickname, avatar and posting options so you can choose privacy and interaction settings for each post. It sends only the clips and captions you choose.

  • Posts from the editor are sent only after you choose the settings and confirm.
  • Scheduled posts are sent to your TikTok inbox as drafts for you to finish in TikTok.
  • TikTok data is stored only on your PC. Disconnecting TikTok in PeakSense revokes the token.
  • You can also remove PeakSense in TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions.

See TikTok's Privacy Policy.

9. The website (peaksense.net)

peaksense.net is a static site. If you join the launch list, your email address is collected by our list provider, LaunchList, which also loads a small script on the page. We use the address only to email you once when PeakSense launches.

If you email us, we use your message only to reply. Our web host receives standard request information, such as your IP address and browser type, to serve the pages. We don't use analytics or advertising cookies.

To be removed from the list or have your emails deleted, write to support@peaksense.net. We act within 30 days.

10. Your choices and rights

  • Disconnect a platform in PeakSense (Accounts). This revokes and deletes its token.
  • Revoke access at the platform:
    • Google: security settings
    • Twitch: Settings → Connections
    • TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions
  • Delete your data: follow Data deletion. Because the app's data lives only on your PC, deleting it there deletes it completely.
  • Access, correction and deletion requests for anything we hold (launch-list email, support emails): write to support@peaksense.net. Depending on where you live (for example under GDPR or US state privacy laws) you may have further rights, including to complain to your data-protection authority. We honour them.

11. Children

PeakSense is not directed at children. You must be at least 13 years old, and old enough to use each platform you connect under its own rules, to use PeakSense. We don't knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child sent us their email, contact us and we'll delete it.
