PEAKSENSE — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

PeakSense ("we", "our", "the app") is a content creation and cross-posting tool that helps creators identify, edit, and publish highlight clips from their own gameplay recordings to the social platforms they choose to connect. This Privacy Policy explains what information PeakSense handles and how.

1. What Information We Handle

PeakSense runs locally on the creator's own computer. Depending on which features you use, it may handle:

  • Video files you record or download for processing (e.g., stream recordings, gameplay footage)
  • Basic account information from platforms you connect (e.g., your display name and channel/profile ID)
  • OAuth authentication tokens issued by platforms you explicitly connect (YouTube, Twitch, TikTok)
  • Metadata you enter for posts, such as titles and descriptions

2. How We Use It

This information is used solely to operate the app's core functionality: identifying highlight moments in your footage, rendering edited clips, and — only when you explicitly initiate it — publishing those clips to a platform you've connected under your own account.

3. Where Your Data Is Stored

PeakSense does not operate a remote server that stores your videos or account credentials. Video files, authentication tokens, and app settings are stored locally on your own computer. We do not upload your footage or tokens to any PeakSense-operated infrastructure.

4. Launch List and Contact

If you join the launch list on peaksense.net or email us, we receive your email address. We use it only to tell you when PeakSense is available (for the launch list) or to reply to you. We don't sell it, share it, or send marketing beyond that. To be removed at any time, email support@peaksense.net.

5. Third-Party Platforms

When you choose to post content, PeakSense sends that content directly to the platform you've connected (e.g., TikTok, YouTube) using that platform's official API, under the authorization you've granted. Each platform's use of your data is governed by that platform's own privacy policy:

  • TikTok: https://www.tiktok.com/legal/page/row/privacy-policy/en
  • YouTube/Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Twitch: https://www.twitch.tv/p/legal/privacy-notice/

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with any other third party, and we do not use your data for advertising.

6. Revoking Access

You can disconnect PeakSense from any platform at any time:

  • TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Manage apps and devices
  • YouTube/Google: Google Account permissions
  • Twitch: Settings → Connections

Disconnecting revokes PeakSense's access token for that platform. Deleting the app from your computer removes all locally stored data.

7. Data Security

Because data is stored locally on your own device rather than in the cloud, you are responsible for the physical and digital security of that device. We recommend keeping your operating system and PeakSense up to date.

8. Children's Privacy

PeakSense is not directed at children and is not intended for use by anyone who does not meet the minimum age requirements of the platforms it connects to (e.g., TikTok, YouTube).

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

10. Contact Us

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be sent to support@peaksense.net.
