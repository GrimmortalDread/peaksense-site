6. Posting

Manual posting: when you post from the editor, you review the clip, title and caption first. Instagram and Facebook Reels are posted publicly. For TikTok you also choose the privacy level and interaction settings (nothing is preselected), declare commercial content where it applies, and confirm before anything is sent.

Scheduled posting (PeakSense Pro): if you set posting days and times, you authorize PeakSense to upload clips from your posting queue to your connected account at those times.

  • YouTube: clips are uploaded with the privacy and scheduling setting you chose. You can cancel or change a queued post before it goes out, or turn on manual approval so nothing posts without your click.
  • TikTok: scheduled clips are only sent to your TikTok inbox as drafts. You finish and publish them in TikTok yourself.
  • Instagram and Facebook: clips are published as Reels with the caption generated or written for them. Instagram only allows posting to Creator or Business accounts. Facebook posts go to the Page you selected. You can cancel a queued post before it goes out, or turn on manual approval.

Until PeakSense completes each platform's app review, posts may be restricted to private visibility by that platform, and each platform may limit how many posts can be made per day.
