PEAKSENSE — Data Deletion

Last updated: September 25, 2026

PeakSense runs on your computer and doesn't keep your videos, tokens or account data anywhere but your PC, so there is no PeakSense account or server copy to delete. Our sign-in relay for TikTok and Twitch passes requests through without storing anything. Your data lives on your PC. Deleting it there removes it completely.

1. Revoke PeakSense's access to your accounts

  1. In PeakSense, open Accounts and click Disconnect for each platform. This revokes PeakSense's token with that platform and deletes it from your PC.
  2. To be sure, you can also remove PeakSense at each platform:
    • Google / YouTube: security.google.com/settings/security/permissions
    • Twitch: Settings → Connections → PeakSense → Disconnect
    • TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions → PeakSense → Remove

2. Delete the data on your PC

  1. Close PeakSense (including its tray icon).
  2. Delete these folders:
    • %LOCALAPPDATA%\PeakSense: the app's database, settings, clips, logs, sign-in tokens and its sign-in window's data;
    • %APPDATA%\PeakSense: YouTube sign-in files;
    • the download / library folder you set in PeakSense, if you want to remove downloaded or copied videos;
    • any _peaksense_kept folders next to your recordings (720p copies and kept clip footage) and *.live_chat.json files next to OBS recordings (chat logs).
  3. Uninstall PeakSense from Windows Settings → Apps.

Your own recordings and imported videos stay where you keep them. PeakSense never moves them, so delete them yourself if you want them gone.

3. Data we hold

The only personal data we hold is:

  • your email address, if you joined the launch list (kept by our list provider, LaunchList);
  • emails you sent us.

To delete it, email support@peaksense.net with the subject "Data deletion request" from the address concerned. We'll delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.
