PEAKSENSE — Data Deletion

Last updated: November 4, 2026

PeakSense runs on your computer and keeps your videos, tokens and account data only on your PC, so there is no PeakSense account or server copy of them to delete. Deleting PeakSense's data on your PC removes it completely. The few things our relay can hold are listed in section 4, with how to have them deleted.

Deleting PeakSense's data never deletes anything on YouTube, TikTok or any other platform: your videos, playlists, captions and comments there stay as they are. Delete those on the platform itself.

1. Disconnect in PeakSense (deletes what came from YouTube and TikTok)

  1. In PeakSense, open Settings → Connections and click Disconnect on each platform's card.
  2. This deletes the platform's sign-in token from your PC and, where the platform supports it (YouTube, TikTok, Facebook), revokes PeakSense's access there too.
  3. For YouTube and TikTok, disconnecting also deletes, at once, what PeakSense got from them: post statistics and view readings, video and playlist lists, channel details, comments and YouTube watch-time reports. Your clips, montages and settings stay.
  4. To remove only one YouTube extra (Title & playlist edits, or Watch time), click Remove next to it on the YouTube card.

2. Or remove PeakSense at each platform

  • Google / YouTube: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. PeakSense then deletes the YouTube data it kept 7 days after it finds its access removed (so you can sign in again first if you didn't mean to).
  • Twitch: Settings → Connections → PeakSense → Disconnect
  • TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions → PeakSense → Remove
  • Instagram: Settings → Website permissions → Apps and websites → PeakSense → Remove
  • Facebook: Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites → PeakSense → Remove (for Pages: Business integrations)

3. Delete everything on your PC

  1. Disconnect your accounts first (section 1), so PeakSense's access is revoked at each platform.
  2. Close PeakSense (including its tray icon).
  3. Delete these folders:
    • %LOCALAPPDATA%\PeakSense: the app's database, settings, clips, logs and its window's data;
    • %APPDATA%\PeakSense: sign-in files;
    • the library folder you set in PeakSense, if you want to remove the videos PeakSense recorded, downloaded from your Twitch channel or copied;
    • any _peaksense_kept folders next to your recordings (720p copies and kept clip footage) and *.live_chat.json files next to OBS recordings (chat logs).
  4. Uninstall PeakSense from Windows Settings → Apps.

Your own recordings and imported videos stay where you keep them. PeakSense never moves them, so delete them yourself if you want them gone.

4. Data we hold, and how to have it deleted

We hold only:

  • if YouTube is connected: your channel ID and the IDs of your newest public videos, on our relay for new-video notifications (up to 30 days; it ends on its own within 30 days of disconnecting YouTube);
  • crash reports you sent or allowed (kept 90 days; no names, file paths or account details) and, if you turned them on, anonymous YouTube usage counts (no IDs at all);
  • if you bought Pro: your Pro key under a random install ID, for 30 days, so the app can activate it;
  • if you added the PeakSense Discord bot: its settings for your server and your last 10 announced posts (removing the bot in the app deletes them);
  • your email address, if you joined the launch list (kept by our list provider, LaunchList), and emails you sent us.

To have any of it deleted, email support@peaksense.net with the subject "Data deletion request" from the address concerned (for the relay, include your YouTube channel's link). We delete it as soon as possible and within 7 days, and confirm by email.
