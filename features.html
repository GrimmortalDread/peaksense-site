Features

Everything below is in the free version unless it says Pro. Pro adds the things that happen without a click.

Detection

How PeakSense decides what's worth clipping.

The signals chat reacts to

Loud spikes, funny lines, hype phrases and chat blowing up all feed one score, so the clips you get back are the ones worth posting.

Kills and clutches from your HUD

Kills, headshots, multi-kills, clutches, wins and boss fights, read from your game's HUD and sounds. Set up a game in a few minutes from your own footage.

Watches your stream with OBS

Connect OBS once, no plugin. When you go live, PeakSense records and marks moments as they happen. When you stop, the recording is ready to clip.

Mark it yourself

A hotkey, !clip in chat, a Stream Deck button, or the big Mark button on your phone.

Keyword search

Type a word like "clutch" or "GG" and PeakSense finds every time you said it, including words that sound alike, then clips just those.

Optional local AI scoring

Runs through Ollama on your own machine. Small Gemma models work on a modest PC; bigger ones on a big GPU.

Know who's talking

On co-streams and podcasts, each line is labelled with the speaker. Optional, needs a free Hugging Face account.

Never the same moment twice

Process a stream again and PeakSense skips what it already clipped. With Pro, it takes a second look a few hours later on its own.

Editing

Finish a clip in the same app. No exporting, no subscription.

Facecam layouts

Webcam on top and gameplay below, camera on the bottom, side-by-side, or a two-cam duo. Your webcam is found for you and stays lined up for the whole stream.

Burned-in captions

Word-by-word styles you can preview live, or set the font, size, colours and position yourself.

Trim, cut and speed ramps

Pull extra footage from the stream, skip the slow middle, slow down the play and speed through the walk back.

Zoom and pan

Punch in on the kill while your facecam stays put.

Text, hooks and music

Titles and callouts, a one-tap hook line your local AI writes, filters, fades and background music.

Sequences and montages

Stitch clips into one video with intro and outro cards, one layout for every clip.

Review mode

New clips play full screen, one key each to keep, queue, bin, star or edit.

Re-cut anything

Change the quality, layout or aspect ratio of any clip without starting over.

Posting

Where your clips go, and how.

YouTube

Sign in with Google and post Shorts from the editor with the title and description filled in. Scheduled posting, title testing and playlists come with Pro.

Instagram and Facebook

Post Reels to a Creator or Business account, and to your Facebook Page. Scheduled with Pro.

TikTok

Sign in with TikTok and post from the editor. Scheduled clips go to your drafts for you to publish.

A posting queue

Line clips up, drag them onto calendar days, and see what went out in the Activity timeline.

Your phone as a remote

Over Tailscale, your phone gets the dashboard and a Mark button for when you're live. Free notifications through the ntfy app.

Twitch while you're live Pro

Raids, channel points, bits and chat spikes become clips on their own, then captioned Shorts once you go offline.

Your PC, your files

What stays on your machine.

Nothing gets uploaded

Recording, detection, clipping and captions all run on your PC. PeakSense only goes online to check your own channel or post to your own accounts.

One library for every stream

OBS recordings, files you drop in, watched folders (Studio downloads, ShadowPlay, ReLive) and past broadcasts from your own channel.

Keeps your drive in check

Pin streams to keep, shrink processed ones to about a tenth of the size, and get warned before a job runs out of space.

MKV to MP4, no re-encode

Record to MKV so a crash can't lose the stream. PeakSense converts it when you stop, with no quality loss.

A stream clipper that runs on your own Windows PC. Free, with a one-time Pro.
